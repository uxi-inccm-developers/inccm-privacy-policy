1Who we are

INCCM - Booking Scanner, also called MIS (the "App"), is the staff app of the INC Children's Museum ("INCCM", "we", "us" or "our"), the children's museum inside the INC Museum in Quezon City, Philippines. Museum staff at the receiving area use it to check guests in by scanning their booking QR codes, to check and manage bookings, and to add bookings for walk-in guests.

The App is not for the public. Only staff with an account that the museum has created for them can sign in, and every feature needs that sign-in. Guests book their visits in the INCCM App, which has its own privacy policy.

UXI Information Technology Solutions develops the App and publishes it on app stores on behalf of the INC Children's Museum.

This policy explains what information the App collects about the staff who use it and the guests whose bookings it shows, how we use that information, and the rights you have. We handle personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

3The camera and QR codes

The App asks for permission to use the camera so that staff can scan booking QR codes. The camera is on only while the Scanner is open and the App is in front, and it reads QR codes only. The pictures are processed on the device as they come in: the App doesn't take or save photos or videos, and doesn't send them anywhere. Only the text of a QR code it reads is sent to our servers, to find the booking.

On Android, the App reads QR codes with Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device. ML Kit may send Google information about the device, the App and how well scanning performs, for diagnostics, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure. It doesn't send camera pictures. On iOS, the App uses Apple's built-in camera features.

The device vibrates briefly when a QR code is read. The App remembers which camera (front or back) the Scanner uses.

4How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • sign staff in, keep them signed in and sign them out;
  • confirm bookings and check guests in when they arrive at the museum;
  • show, add, edit, cancel and delete bookings, and prepare for each group's visit;
  • contact guests about their bookings;
  • keep the App and our servers secure and working, and prevent misuse;
  • comply with our legal obligations.

We process this information to provide the visits guests book with us, because we have a legitimate interest in running the museum's bookings and keeping them secure, or because the law requires us to. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to build profiles of staff or guests.

5Information on the device

When you sign in, the App keeps your email address, your account's ID and role, the time you signed in and a sign-in token (so you stay signed in) in the device's secure storage: the Keychain on iOS, or encrypted with the Android Keystore on Android. Signing out removes them.

The App also keeps the email address you last signed in with, so the next sign-in starts with it, and the Scanner's camera choice. These stay on the device after you sign out, until the App is deleted. On a device that staff share, the next person can see the last email address used.

Guests' booking details are kept only in the App's memory while it is showing them, and are cleared when you sign out, when your sign-in ends, or when the App closes. The App doesn't save them on the device. What the App does keep isn't copied to other devices through backups. On iOS, the system may keep the sign-in and preferences after the App is deleted, until it is reinstalled and you sign out.

6How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • With museum staff: authorized INC Museum and INC Children's Museum personnel who handle bookings and visits.
  • With service providers: companies that host our servers or help us run the App. They may use the information only on our instructions and must keep it confidential and secure.
  • With Google, on Android: the diagnostic information ML Kit sends, described in The camera and QR codes.
  • When required by law: to comply with a law, regulation, court order or other legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our visitors, our staff or the public.

Google Play and the Apple App Store deliver the App and its updates, and may collect information under their own privacy policies.

7Guests' information

Most bookings are made by guests in the INCCM App; staff use this App to see and manage them. When a guest books at the museum instead, staff enter the guest's details in this App for them. If the email address belongs to an INCCM App account, the booking is linked to that account.

Bookings record how many toddlers, children and guardians are in a group, but not the children's names. The App is used by adults on the museum's staff, and children don't use it.

Guests have the rights described in Your rights, and can contact us about their booking information at any time.

For staff: use guests' information only for museum work. Don't copy, photograph or share it outside the booking system, and don't put sensitive information, such as health details or government ID numbers, in a booking's notes.

8Security

Only staff with an account can sign in, and the App works only while they are signed in. The App sends passwords only in hashed form and keeps the sign-in in the device's secure storage. When our server ends a sign-in, the App signs out and clears the bookings it was showing.

We use reasonable organizational, physical and technical measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access, and we limit access to the people who need it to handle bookings. No method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure, however, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

On a shared device, sign out at the end of your shift, and don't leave the device unattended while you're signed in.

9How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected for:

  • Staff accounts, for as long as you need the account for your work at the museum;
  • Sign-in records and records of changes to bookings, only as long as needed to keep the booking system secure and answer questions about bookings;
  • Booking information, for as long as needed to manage the booking and visit, and afterwards only as long as required for record-keeping or legal purposes.

After that, we delete the information or make it anonymous.

10Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, staff and guests have the right to:

  • be informed about how their personal information is processed;
  • access the personal information we hold about them;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected;
  • object to processing, or withdraw their consent;
  • have their information deleted or blocked;
  • get a copy of their information in a commonly used electronic format;
  • be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorized use of their information;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Making a request

Email us at info@incmuseum.com. Guests: include your booking reference number and the email address you booked with. Staff: email us from your account's email address. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request. If a guest asks us to delete their booking details before their visit, we may not be able to keep the booking.

11Your staff account

The museum creates and closes the App's accounts; you can't create one in the App. To close your account, for example when you no longer work at the receiving area, ask your supervisor or email info@incmuseum.com from your account's email address, and we'll close it and delete your account details.

Records of your sign-ins and of changes you made to bookings may be kept after your account is closed, as described in How long we keep information. Signing out removes your sign-in from the device; deleting the App also removes the email address it remembers.

If a change affects what information we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect and, where the law requires, ask for consent. Please check this page from time to time.
