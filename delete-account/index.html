Delete your account

Updated October 22, 2026 · Part of the INCCM App Privacy Policy

If you created an account in the INCCM App, the app of the INC Children's Museum (published on Google Play and the App Store by UXI Information Technology Solutions), you can delete it at any time: in the App, or by asking us by email. Deleting your account is permanent and can't be undone.

The short version

  • In the App: Menu → Settings → Delete account, then enter your password.
  • Without the App: email reachus@uxi.rocks from your account's email address.
  • Deleted: your account and its profile.
  • Kept: booking details and Chat messages, which aren't part of your account, for the times listed below, unless you ask us to delete them too.

1Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the INCCM App. If you aren't signed in, sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. On the home screen, tap the menu button (☰) at the bottom left.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  5. Enter your password, then tap Delete account.

Your account is deleted right away. The App then signs you out and removes your account details from your device.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? to have a temporary password emailed to you, sign in with it, and then delete your account. Or ask us to delete it.

2Ask us to delete your account

If you no longer have the App or can't sign in, ask us instead. You don't need to install the App:

  1. Email reachus@uxi.rocks from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete my INCCM App account".
  2. If we need to confirm that the account is yours, we'll reply and ask you to.
  3. We delete your account and email you when it's done.

3What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete:

  • your account: your first, middle and last name, suffix, birthday, gender, email address and password;
  • your profile: whether you are an INC member, and your INC district.

When you delete your account in the App, the App also removes the account details and sign-in that it keeps in your device's secure storage.

4What we keep, and for how long

Some information isn't part of your account, because you can give it without signing in. Deleting your account doesn't delete it:

Information How long we keep it
Booking details: the names, email address and mobile number you gave, and your visit details As long as needed to manage the booking and your visit, and afterwards only as long as required for record-keeping or legal purposes
Chat messages with Museum Support, with the name, email address and device identifier sent with them As long as needed to answer you and keep a record of support requests
IP addresses and technical details of requests to our servers Only as long as needed to keep our servers secure and fix problems

If the law requires us to keep some information longer, for example for a legal claim, we keep only that information, and only for as long as required. After that, we delete information or make it anonymous.

5Delete other information

To have your booking details or Chat messages deleted, with or without your account, email reachus@uxi.rocks with your booking reference number (for bookings) or the name and email address you used in Chat. If you ask before your visit, we may not be able to keep your booking.

Read more about your rights in our Privacy Policy.
